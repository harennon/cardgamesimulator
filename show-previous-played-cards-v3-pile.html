Trick Pile — Collapsed Stack Beside the Current Play (v3)

A physical-looking pile of cards sits next to the currently played hand. Click it to fan out and review every play in the current trick, in order. It resets when a new trick starts.

What this is (per the latest direction):
  • A collapsed stacked pile (face-down card backs, layered & slightly fanned so it reads as real cards) positioned beside the current play. A gold badge shows how many plays are in the trick.
  • Click / tap the pile to expand — it reveals all plays of the current trick in order, including pass markers. Click the close button, tap outside, or press Esc to collapse.
  • Resets when a new trick starts (all others pass and the trick winner leads again). Use the demo bar to add plays / passes and to start a new trick.
  • Big2 only. Derived client-side from Big2PublicState.playHistory sliced to the current trick. No backend changes.
