Part 1 · In-game desktop side rail (grid-area "log", 280px)

Tallies vs. Game Log in the side rail

The acceptance criteria require tallies to stay accessible AND the move history to appear on desktop. Two genuinely different structural approaches below. Mobile keeps its existing hamburger log drawer either way.

Option A Segmented toggle

One panel, two views. A segmented control at the top switches between Tallies and Game Log. Full height for whichever is active — the log gets the whole rail to breathe, matching the mobile drawer's density. Remembered per-session so it doesn't reset each turn.

Part 2 · Game-over screen

Showing the final move after a Tonk game ends

Big2's game-over shows a "Final Play" card block. Tonk's final action is a TONK call or a stock-out, followed by a trick result that reveals every hand and the tally deltas. Two treatments: a focused single "final move" block (Big2-parallel), or an expandable recent-history list.

Option 1 Final-move block (Big2 parallel)

Minimal, mirrors Big2. Shows only the last action and its trick outcome: who called TONK, their revealed hand, and the deltas that ended the game. One glance answers "what happened."

It also reuses TonkLog rows, so there's near-zero net-new rendering.
