Discard pile fit on mobile — three approaches

On a narrow viewport, a 5-card current play (full house, straight, flush, four-of-a-kind, straight flush) renders in a fixed .play-area__card-row wider than the table, and overflow: clip on the mobile board cuts off the rightmost cards. Use the controls to reproduce the bug (Baseline) and compare the three candidate fixes across viewport widths and play types.

How to read this

Each phone renders the same mobile game board. The Baseline column is current production behaviour — switch to Full House (5) at 320px and the 5th card is clipped at the felt edge (the bug). The three option columns each keep the play fully on-screen, centered, with the trick pile pinned left.

Option A — Scale-to-fit: measure available width, apply transform: scale() only when the natural row overflows. Cards stay full size for 1–4 card plays; 5-card plays shrink just enough to fit. Preserves a single straight line. GPU-composited, no reflow.
Option B — Dedicated on-table size: the on-table play uses a smaller fixed card size (e.g. --card-play-width, already defined but unused) so 5 cards always fit at any supported width. Simplest; consistent look but on-table cards are always smaller than the hand.
Option C — Wrap: add flex-wrap: wrap so a 5-card play breaks onto a second centered row when it can't fit. No scaling; cards stay full size, but the play occupies two rows and the vertical center shifts.
