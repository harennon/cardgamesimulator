LLD 72 · Frontend Mockup

Game-Over Reveal — show the final cards, lose the timer

Two changes. (1) The reveal phase no longer auto-advances on a 4s timer — it waits for you to click Continue to Results (approved direction B; no time pressure = "I want longer"). (2) The real reported bug: on the reveal screen the final played cards were buried under a full-screen dim + blur overlay, especially on mobile. The banner is reworked into a top ribbon + spotlight so the final play stays the visual hero.

04 Decisions baked into this mockup
  • Timer: removed from the reveal phase entirely (direction B). SHOW_FINAL_PLAY persists until "Continue to Results" is clicked. The shrink progress-bar is gone.
  • Named constant: no timer literal at all in the chosen path. (If a fallback auto-advance is ever wanted, it would be a named constant — not an inline 4000.)
  • The real fix: reveal banner = top ribbon + radial spotlight instead of full-screen dim+blur, so the final cards stay legible on desktop AND mobile.
  • Final play, restyled: the table's current play is promoted into a gold-ringed "Winning Play" card during the reveal — no new data needed, it's the existing lastPlay.
  • Reconnect: joining an already-COMPLETED game still skips straight to results (no reveal phase) — unchanged from LLD 43.
  • Optional results-screen row: small final-play row on GameOverView for users who reach results and still want to see the cards.
  • Reduced motion: deal-in / lift animations gated behind prefers-reduced-motion; cards appear instantly, fully legible.
