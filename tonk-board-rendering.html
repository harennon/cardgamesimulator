The core challenge. Tonk discards before drawing, so the live discard top is the current player's own just-played card — NOT what a drawing player may take. The board must show two different cards at once: the live pile top (Q♠, "Devon just played") and the drawable snapshot (7♥, "from Maya", cyan ring). Sections 2–3 show distinct ways to render that distinction.
Decision A

Drawable-discard indicator — three treatments

How to make it obvious which card a drawing player could take from the discard (the turn-start drawableDiscard snapshot, distinct from the live pile top). This is the one genuinely new visual problem in Tonk. Pick one — they are genuinely different mental models. A1 (separate slot) reads most clearly for new players.

A1 · Separate drawable slot recommended
Drawable card lifted out beside the pile, cyan-ringed, labeled. The pile shows the live top untouched. Two distinct objects = least ambiguity.

Decision B

Two-phase turn indicator

Tonk turns are two phases — discard then draw — same player throughout. The board must show both whose turn it is AND which phase. B1 (color-coded chip) recommended: one chip in the center banner + a matching tag on the active seat, reusing Big2's pulse-dot turn affordance.

B1 · Color-coded phase chip recommended
Center banner names the player + a colored phase chip. The active seat repeats the phase tag so the eye-line at the rail is enough.

Decision C

Seating at 3–8 players

The rail is a centered, wrapping flex row (matches the uncapped OpponentRow.vue). At 6–8 players seats compress: card-back fans shrink to a single stub + count, names ellipsize, and the tally chip stays. At ≥7 the rail wraps to two rows. This keeps every seat's count + tally + turn state readable without scrolling. Recommended: compact seat at ≥6 (drop the full card fan, keep count + tally).

Reuse

Component reuse plan

Most of the board is the existing Big2 skeleton with a Tonk-specific center. Concrete component-spec details go in the LLD after a direction is picked.

  • Reuse as-is: GameCard.vue (card faces + backs; Joker rendered as a special face), the seat/turn-dot/pulse affordance pattern from OpponentRow.vue, and the auto-scrolling GameLog.vue shell.
  • Adapt: OpponentRow.vue gains a per-seat tally chip + optional phase tag, and a compact mode at ≥6 players (drop the card fan). Could be a Tonk-local TonkSeatRail.vue rather than overloading Big2's row.
  • New, Tonk-only: center TonkPiles (stock + discard + drawable slot), the TonkTallyPanel (lower-is-better + 150 line), and the phase chip. The GameLog needs Tonk entry shapes (discard / draw-source / TONK / trick result) — drive it off the Tonk log, not Big2's playHistory.
  • Dispatch: GameView.vue renders <TonkBoard> when gameType === "tonk", else <GameBoard>. No Tonk branches in the WebSocket/state plumbing — selection is at the component layer only.
  • Read-only: no ActionPanel, no card selection/interactivity. Hand cards render non-interactive (Big2's interactive=false path).
