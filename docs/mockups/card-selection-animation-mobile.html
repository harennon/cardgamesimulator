Fix A: rAF + will-change approved
Wrapping selectedIndices.value = next inside requestAnimationFrame() guarantees the DOM update lands on a fresh compositor frame. Combined with will-change: transform for GPU layer promotion, the lift paints immediately on the next vsync.
