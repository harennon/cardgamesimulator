Card Deal & Play Animations

Presentation-only motion for two moments in the core loop: dealing your hand at round start, and a play landing in the center. Timings are brief (240–260 ms) and GPU-composited (transform / opacity only). Use the controls to trigger each moment and compare variants. Same tokens as the live board (felt, gold, serif card faces). Works identically for Big2 and Tonk hands/piles.

Recommended default: Deal A (Slide up) + Play 1 (Drop). Slide-up reads clearly on mobile with no horizontal overflow risk, and Drop is the most legible "a play just landed" cue without implying a specific source position. Variant B (deck fly) is the most characterful but assumes a fixed deck anchor and can look off on the Tonk board where cards come from stock/discard piles, not a single deck.

What animates (scoped intentionally): only your own hand's deal-in and the play area's landing row. Opponent card-backs, counts, the game log, and the turn timer are unchanged — animating every state change would be noisy and heavier. On the Tonk board the same deal-in applies to TonkHand; the play landing maps to the discard pile top in TonkPiles.

Reduced motion: toggling the switch above adds body.reduced-motion, which sets animation: none — cards render instantly, exactly as today. In production this is driven by the @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) query (same pattern as TurnTimer.vue).
