Tonk — Seeing your own score

Today a Tonk player cannot see their own tally: the seat rail deliberately excludes self, and on mobile the tally panel is hidden entirely with no drawer. Since Tonk ends when anyone hits 150, knowing your own standing is essential to deciding whether to play conservatively or call TONK. Below are three distinct ways to surface the local player's own tally, persistently, on both desktop and mobile.

All values are read from the same server-authoritative tonkState.tallies[myPlayerIndex] the tally panel and seat rail already use — no client-side score recomputation.

Option A Recommended

Self-seat chip in the rail

Add the local player back into the seat rail as a distinct "You" chip (cyan accent, always first). This puts your tally right beside opponents' tallies, using the exact surface players already scan for standings — one consistent comparison row. Because the rail is the only per-player UI shown on mobile, this single change fixes desktop and mobile at once. The card-back fan is dropped for self (you see your real hand below), keeping the chip compact.

Why recommended: smallest surface-area change, reuses the rail that already renders on every viewport, and keeps self/opponent tallies in one glanceable row. The cyan "You" treatment doubles as the self-seat affordance the rail lacks today.

Option B

"Your score" pill on the hand label row

Keep the seat rail opponents-only, but enrich the existing "Your hand (n)" label row with a right-aligned score pill. Your tally lives directly above your cards — where your eyes already are while deciding a move. The pill turns amber when you cross the near-150 warning threshold (120+), mirroring the tally panel's warning styling. Works identically on desktop and mobile since the hand zone is always present on both.

Trade-off: puts the score in the most action-relevant spot (next to your hand) but does not place it beside opponents' tallies, so direct standings comparison still requires the rail/panel.

Option C

Floating corner HUD badge

A small floating badge anchored to the lower-left corner of the board, hovering just above the hand zone. It shows your tally large, with a "/ 150" reference and a mini progress-to-loss-line bar — the same gauge metaphor the tally panel uses. It is the most prominent/glanceable of the three and never competes with the rail or hand label, but it introduces a new floating element and overlaps the felt (a bit more visual chrome).

Trade-off: highest prominence and richest self-context (bar + loss line), but adds a floating overlay to a board that otherwise keeps chrome minimal, and needs care so it never covers cards on the smallest viewports.
