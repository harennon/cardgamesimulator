Quick-Emote Table Reactions

Tap-to-send ephemeral reactions that float over the sender's seat for everyone at the table — players and spectators. No free-text chat: a fixed, curated palette spanning supportive hype and light competitive banter. Click emotes below to see them animate; try the cooldown and the "hide emotes" toggle.
