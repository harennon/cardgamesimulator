Rematch / Play Again — Game Over Screen

Three directions for turning the dead "Rematch (Coming soon)" button into a working flow. All preserve the existing felt-table panel, score table, gold-accent buttons, and the guest sign-up nudge. The directions differ in the multiplayer coordination model — that is the real product decision, and it drives the server-side rematch flow in the LLD.

What you are choosing: not just a button style, but how a rematch is coordinated across players. Option A = instant host-driven (one click, everyone is pulled in). Option B = opt-in ready-up (each present player accepts; a roster shows who is in). Option C = lightweight host-driven with an auto-route (host clicks, others get a brief "starting…" then land in the new game). Each option below also shows the relevant button/roster states.
Option A

Instant Rematch — host-driven, one click

The host sees an active Rematch button. One click creates a new round with everyone still present and immediately routes all connected players into it (reusing the same room code). Non-host players see a passive "host can start a rematch" state, then get pulled in automatically. Simplest model, lowest friction — matches the existing host-only "Start Game" pattern in the lobby. Players who left are simply dropped; the new round starts with whoever is still here (min 2).

Option B

Ready-Up Rematch — opt-in, every player accepts

Any present player can hit Play Again. Doing so marks them "ready" and reveals a roster showing who has accepted, who is still deciding, and who already left. The new round starts automatically once all still-present players are ready (host can also force-start with the ready subset, min 2). Most explicit and fair for casual groups — no one is yanked into a game they didn't agree to. Players who left the table show as grayed and are excluded. Highest clarity, slightly more friction.

Option C

Quick Rematch — host-driven with countdown route

Middle ground. The host hits Rematch; instead of an instant yank (A) or a full ready-up (B), every present player gets a short "New round starting in 3…" toast with a chance to bail to Home. Anyone who doesn't leave is routed into the new round. Keeps host control and low friction, but gives non-host players a beat of warning and an exit — good for avoiding the "wait, I wasn't ready" complaint of pure Option A. Departed players are excluded.
