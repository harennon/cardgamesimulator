Tonk — Round-End Card Review Pause

When a Tonk round ends (a player calls TONK, or the stock runs out) the engine immediately deals the next round. Today the only place the revealed hands appear is the game log (a drawer on mobile), so players never get a beat to see who won/lost the round. These mockups add a reveal overlay that shows every player's revealed hand + the points each seat took, before the next round's board becomes interactive.

The board underneath each panel is intentionally the already-dealt next round — the overlay is what buys the review pause. Three distinct directions below, then a mobile view and the two end reasons (TONK vs stock-out).
